**Snapshot taken 20 September 2026** by loading every page in a browser and recording what it actually

## Before you start

**How to edit**

1. Change the words directly where they are. Leave the bold labels (**Heading:**, **Text:** and so on) as
   they are, so each change can be matched to its exact place on the site.
2. To leave a question or an instruction instead of rewriting, put it on its own line starting with
   **NOTE:**. For example: `NOTE: remove this sentence` or `NOTE: can this mention the river?`
3. When you're done, send the file back. I'll compare it with this original, make every change on the site,
   and show you the result on the page at phone and laptop sizes.

**Things to know while reading**

- **Capitals.** Small labels and buttons are shown in CAPITALS on the site. That is a design style. They are
  typed normally here, and you should type them normally too.
- **The soft word in headings.** Every main heading shows one word in a lighter tone, for example "Two
  forests, known *deeply*". Each heading here lists its soft word underneath. If you rewrite a heading, say
  which word should be the soft one. It has to appear exactly once in the heading.
- **Shared text.** Some text appears in more than one place: the six Experience cards (on all three pages),
  the contact details, and the lodge names and regions. Each piece is written out once, where it first
  appears, and marked **Shared**. Edit it there and the change shows up everywhere it is used.
- **Hidden text.** Some words aren't visible on the page. Screen readers (used by blind visitors), Google
  search results and the browser tab use them instead. They're marked *(hidden)*. They are worth editing
  too, especially the Google descriptions.
- **Numbers are automatic.** Section numbers ("01", "02"…) are assigned automatically. You only edit the
  words beside them.
- **Typography.** Don't worry about long dashes, curly apostrophes or spacing. I'll match the site's style.

**Where length matters.** Most text can grow or shrink freely, and I check every change on screen. These
places are tight:

- **The big opening headline on each page** sits over a photograph in very large type. Keep it to about its
  current length: one short phrase of 35 to 40 characters, with no full stop.
- **The six Experience cards and the two lodge panels on Home** have their words laid on a photograph. Keep
  each card to one short sentence.
- **Section labels** (such as "The Lodges") and **buttons**: a few words.

## 5. Worth checking while you edit

These came up while putting this file together. None of them has been changed, because each one is your
decision.

### Two room details contradict themselves

- **Tola, Suite:** the description says "A **king** bed under bamboo", but the facts line says "**Queen**
  bed". The old website also says queen.
- **Tola, Super Deluxe Cottage:** it is the only room with no size. The old website lists it at **300 sq ft**
  (it is where the other three Tola sizes came from). Should 300 sq ft be added?

### Words that no longer match their photograph

- **Vann, Cottage without Deck:** the description opens with "A private sit-out under cane", but the
  photograph now shown is cropped to the bed and no longer includes the sit-out. The sit-out is real and is
  still in the facts line, so nothing is false. The description just leads with something the picture
  doesn't show. You could keep it, or lead with the bed.
- **Vann, Cottage with Deck:** its hidden photo description calls the room "a mud-walled **suite**". The room
  is sold as Cottage with Deck.

### Things that may describe the future as the present

- **Home, Mahua Tola panel:** "where three new river-facing machaans look onto a stretch…". The project notes
  from 12 August record these three machaans as **still being built**. If they aren't open yet, the sentence
  describes them as if they were.

### Facts that have never been confirmed

The site states these, but they came from the brand record or the old website and have never been checked
with you:

- **Eight** of the Vann cottages have a deck over the seasonal river (Home, 04 · Mahua Philosophy)
- **More than a hundred** Kumhar families at Pachdhar (Home 03, and Vann 01)
- **Some three hundred** recorded birds (Home 02)
- Tadoba's tiger density is **among the highest in the country** (Home 02, and Tola 01). The old website said
