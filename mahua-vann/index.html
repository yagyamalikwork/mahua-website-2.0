The Forest

The forest of the Jungle Book

Mahua Vann sits in the heart of Pench National Park, close enough to Turia Gate to be among the first vehicles through it. Mornings begin on a jeep safari through forest Kipling wrote into the Jungle Book without ever visiting; afternoons slow down at Kohka Lake, where the birds come to the water and the day comes down with them.

A short drive away, more than a hundred Kumhar families in the village of Pachdhar have kept the potter's wheel turning for generations. Guests are welcome to sit down at one.

The Experience

The day at Vann

Morning and evening game drives, birdwatching in the lodge's own private eco park, and a quieter afternoon at Kohka Lake or the Pachdhar potters' wheel.

  • Fireside

    Private Bush Dinners

    Tables under the trees, a fire going, the forest listening in.

  • Stillness

    Wellness

    Lawn yoga, pranayama and candlelit sound baths.

  • After dark

    Screenings and Star Talks

    Telescopes on the lawn and wildlife documentaries under the trees.

  • On foot

    Nature Walks and Birding

    Guided trails around the lodge — pugmarks, birdcalls, small dramas.

  • Local hands

    Village Craft

    Pottery at the wheel, learnt from the villages next door.

  • At the gate

    Jungle Safari

    Morning and evening drives in open vehicles, led by naturalists who have followed these particular tigresses and their lineages for years.
