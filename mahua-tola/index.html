01

The Reserve

Where Tadoba runs at its densest

Mahua Tola sits at the edge of the Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve, a five-kilometre run from Kolara Gate that puts its jeeps at the front of the queue when the barrier lifts. Tadoba carries one of the highest tiger densities anywhere in the country, and the drives here are guided by trackers who know this forest's cats by name.

The Hattinala river runs along the property — flowing water, birdsong, and a walk with nothing scheduled on it. The villages just beyond the boundary are their own find: a warm welcome, and a bamboo crafts market worth the detour.

The Experience

The day at Tola

Tiger safaris at dawn and dusk, an afternoon along the Hattinala, a bonfire once the light goes, and a pool to come back to between drives.

  • Fireside

    Private Bush Dinners

    Tables under the trees, a fire going, the forest listening in.

  • Stillness

    Wellness

    Lawn yoga, pranayama and candlelit sound baths.

  • After dark

    Screenings and Star Talks

    Telescopes on the lawn and wildlife documentaries under the trees.

  • On foot

    Nature Walks and Birding

    Guided trails around the lodge — pugmarks, birdcalls, small dramas.

  • Local hands

    Village Craft

    Pottery at the wheel, learnt from the villages next door.

  • At the gate

    Jungle Safari

    Morning and evening drives in open vehicles, led by naturalists who have followed these particular tigresses and their lineages for years.
